# MISSION (v1)

## Purpose

Develop and execute a sustainable business model that generates a floor of $250/month USD NET recurring income (no maximum) for the user through ethical activities, operating >80% self-sufficiently on the 4090/128GB/1TB machine with the user as legal account holder.
## Success criteria

- Sustained NET recurring income of at least $250/month USD for the user (floor, no maximum)
- >80% of operational tasks executed self-sufficiently by the agent team on the 4090/128GB/1TB machine
- All activities remain ethical and compliant with applicable laws and platform terms
- User is the legal account holder for all financial accounts and transactions
- Income is recurring/sustainable, not one-time or depleting
## Scope

- Designing, building, and operating income-generating products/services on the 4090/128GB/1TB machine
- Marketing, sales, and delivery of those products/services
- Financial tracking, accounting, and payout management (user as account holder)
- Legal and governance compliance for all activities
- Hiring and managing the lean 5-department agent structure (Leader-approved)
## Non-goals

- Scaling beyond the 4090/128GB/1TB machine's capacity
- Any unethical, illegal, or platform-violating activity
- Treating income above the $250/month floor as a required target (no maximum enforced)
- Micromanaging individual contributors or another sub-agent's direct reports
## Constraints

- Hardware limited to the 4090/128GB/1TB machine
- All activities must be ethical and legally compliant
- User must be the legal account holder for all financial accounts
- Financial risk floor of $250/month USD NET recurring (no maximum)
- Leader is sole editor of MISSION.md and must seek permission before every edit
## Org recommendation

Lean 5-department structure: Revenue Ops, Finance, Engineering & Compute, Legal & Governance, HR (see org_recommendation).
